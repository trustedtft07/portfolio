I

About the author

A short account of who is doing the building, and why.

❦

My name is Ahmad Andhika Haririe. I am reading Informatics Engineering at Universitas Sriwijaya, in Palembang, South Sumatra, and I write software across an unusually wide field for one person: browser front-ends, PHP and JavaScript back-ends, Android and iOS applications from a single Flutter codebase, and 2D games in Godot. The through-line is not a framework — it is a preference for systems that a stranger can open six months later and still understand.

Most of what I know has been learned twice: once in a lecture hall, and once at two in the morning against a deadline. The coursework taught me to model the data before drawing the screens. The game jams — three of them so far — taught me to finish. Eleven months on the staff of the Informatics student association taught me that a design file and a working build are two very different promises.

I am comfortable owning a feature end to end — schema, API, interface, deployment — and I would rather ship something small that works than something large that demonstrates. At the moment I am spending my time on edge computing: this site, and the mail behind my own domain, both run on Cloudflare's network with no origin server at all.

II

The craft

Four kinds of work I take on, and what they actually involve.

❦
  • 01

    Web applications

    Component-driven front-ends in React or Svelte, wired to a Laravel, Node or Workers back-end. Authentication, roles, live data, and an admin surface that a non-engineer can actually operate.

    React · Svelte · Laravel · Tailwind · Vite

  • 02

    Mobile applications

    One Flutter codebase, two stores. Offline-tolerant state, camera and barcode input, local notifications, and payment gateways wired up properly — QRIS included.

    Flutter · Dart · Firebase · Midtrans

  • 03

    APIs & edge services

    REST APIs with sane resources and honest status codes, plus serverless services on Cloudflare Workers backed by D1 and R2 — no origin box to patch, and a cold start you cannot measure.

    Workers · D1 · R2 · REST · MySQL

  • 04

    Games & interactive

    2D games in Godot and Unity: state-machine characters, encounters and wave systems, exported to desktop and Android — some for coursework, some against a forty-eight-hour game-jam clock.

    Godot 4 · GDScript · Unity · Blender

IV

At the university

What the degree looks like away from the lecture timetable: the student association, the committee work, and the weekends spent finishing something.

❦

Most of my time outside class at Universitas Sriwijaya has gone into Himpunan Mahasiswa Informatika — the Informatics student association — first on its creative team, then on the staff. The work is design and production: Figma for the layouts, After Effects for anything that has to move, and an event date attached to every one of them.

  1. June 2024

    Matriculated at Universitas Sriwijaya

    Started the Informatics Engineering programme in Palembang, and moved from writing code for myself to writing it for a marker with a rubric.

  2. October – December 2024

    Creative Team — Himpunan Mahasiswa Informatika

    Three months as a volunteer on the association’s creative team: the visual side of the events the department runs for its own students.

    VolunteerDesign

  3. January 2025

    First game jam

    Global Game Jam Palembang 2025, run with Game Developer Palembang. A weekend, a theme, and a build at the end of it — made in Unity and dressed in Photoshop.

    UnityAdobe Photoshop

    Staff — Himpunan Mahasiswa Informatika

    Eleven months on the association’s staff, part-time and hybrid, in Palembang. Figma and After Effects most weeks, and the ordinary committee discipline of getting a thing agreed, made and published before the event it was made for.

    FigmaAfter EffectsHybrid
