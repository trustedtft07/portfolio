Words

Every passage of running text on the page. Anything left alone keeps the wording that is written in the repository; Restore puts a changed passage back to it. You can also edit these in place: open the page while signed in and click a paragraph.

Lists

The repeated things: the craft cards, the record of the university years, the certificates, the letters, the facts in the margin and the ribbon of tools. Add, remove and reorder them here.

Photographs

Anything dropped here is shrunk to 1,600 pixels in the browser before it is sent, and then kept on Cloudflare’s network under the fingerprint of its own bytes. Photographs are hung from the Lists tab — and one of them can be the portrait on the front page.

    Keys

    The password to this room. Changing it signs every other device out, and takes the place of the one the site was deployed with.

    Ten characters at the very least. A short sentence is easier to type on a phone than a jumble.

    How this works

    Nothing here is written into the repository. The page in public/ stays the printed default; the store on Cloudflare KV holds only what has been changed, and the Worker sets one over the other as the page is served. Deleting an override — Restore, on any field — hands the paragraph back to the file.

    Edits are live within about a minute everywhere in the world; on your own connection they are usually there at once.
